Privacy Policy — WebVid Saver

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WebVid Saver ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary

WebVid Saver is a web browser that lets you download videos from websites that permit downloading. This app does not support downloding from social media's like youtube, instagram,facebook etc. The app does not require an account, and we (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive any personal information about you. The videos you download and your browsing activity stay on your device. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which collects limited device and usage data to serve ads, as described below.

Information the app stores on your device

Videos you choose to download, and any browsing you do inside the app, are stored locally on your device only. This information is never transmitted to us and is not accessible to us. You can delete downloaded videos at any time from within the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we display ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, general location, and ad-interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch (or when required), the app asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier for personalized advertising. You can decline, and you can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account information. The app has no sign-up or login.

Children's privacy

WebVid Saver is not directed to children. It provides access to the open web and is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention

Because we do not collect your data, we do not retain it. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Your choices

You can limit ad personalization via the App Tracking Transparency prompt and iOS Settings, and you can delete downloaded content within the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
