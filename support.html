WebVid Saver — Support Help and contact for the WebVid Saver app. WebVid Saver is a simple web browser that lets you download videos from websites that permit downloading, and save them to your device or Photos gallery. Frequently asked questions How do I download a video? Open the built-in browser, go to a web page that has a video, and play or scroll to it. When a downloadable video is found, a download button appears — tap it to save the video. Your downloads appear in the Downloads tab, where you can play them or save them to Photos. Why won't it download from YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok? Downloading is intentionally blocked on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, and other social and streaming platforms, to respect their terms of service. WebVid Saver only downloads from sites that permit it. Please only download content you have the right to save. A video wouldn't download. Some sites protect their videos or stream them in formats that can't be saved, and some require a login. Those videos can't be downloaded. Try a page that offers the video as a standard file. Where are my downloaded videos? In the Downloads tab inside the app. From there you can play a video, delete it, or use "Save to Photos" to copy it into your device's photo gallery.
